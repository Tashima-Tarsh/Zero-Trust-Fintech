A customer returns with a reference number. Was it the right amount? The right order? Has that reference appeared before? And what is the actual status of the funds? Those questions shouldn't require a chain of screenshots and assumptions.

Mathematical evidence correlation

A UTR is valuable because it identifies a transaction. Its usefulness grows when it can be connected to the right order, amount, recipient and source.

The questions that matter

  • Does the payee VPA match the payment request?
  • Is the amount correct down to the paise?
  • Does the reference conflict with another order?
  • Was the observation inside the permitted window?
  • What did the authorized device journey actually expose?

The distinctions that matter

A browser return is an observation. A customer-provided UTR is an unverified claim. A verified account credit is a financial event. DISHA is designed to keep those states separate so a convincing screen cannot quietly become a completed ledger entry.

A better answer is sometimes: we don't know yet.

Questions worth asking

Clear answers, not slogans.

Does DISHA replace the UPI network or my bank?

No. Customers use their existing UPI applications and the applicable banks move funds. DISHA is software for checkout, merchant operations, evidence capture and reconciliation.

Can an observed UTR alone prove the merchant received money?

No. A UTR can appear with pending or failed transactions. DISHA correlates reference evidence, but only a qualified financial credit source can establish a confirmed credit for the ledger.

Can a merchant use their own receiving UPI addresses?

Yes. Merchants can configure supported personal or bank-issued merchant UPI handles in their workspace. Account ownership and banking restrictions still apply.

Does DISHA charge a percentage of each transaction?

The proposed DISHA commercial model is a one-time software and implementation fee, not a DISHA per-payment platform percentage. Other external costs and contractual arrangements are separately defined.

Is a customer required to install a new application?

No new DISHA customer wallet is required. The checkout uses an existing UPI app or QR payment method, subject to device and application capabilities.
