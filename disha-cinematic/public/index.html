GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

A few things
worth knowing.

Because choosing your payment software is a business decision, not just a design preference.

What exactly am I buying?+

A perpetual license for the agreed DISHA software version, plus the installation and handover services in your quotation. The intellectual property stays with DISHA unless separately agreed.

Do I pay DISHA every month?+

No recurring DISHA software subscription under this model. Your own hosting, third-party network or banking charges, taxes, and optional services remain separate.

How is my price calculated?+

Four months of verified monthly net savings: genuinely removable costs, less new ongoing expenses introduced by your deployment.

What if I already pay zero UPI fees?+

We'll review other genuinely removable software and operating costs. If positive savings cannot be established, we won't invent a savings-based quotation.

Does DISHA guarantee a payment is safe?+

No. The platform includes available transaction and evidence controls to improve visibility and reduce specific operational risks, not a guarantee that fraud cannot occur.

Can I sign up immediately?+

Existing merchants can log in now. New merchant access requires authorized provisioning; use the estimate to understand the commercial model while onboarding is arranged.
