P2M / MERCHANT LANE

Merchant payments.
Separate by design.

A P2M payment is generated in your authenticated merchant workspace using an active merchant-designated receiving UPI ID and its bank-issued MCC. Personal accounts are not accepted in this lane.

STEP 01Merchant accountSign in to your DISHA merchant workspace. Confirm the account is configured as a merchant receiver.
STEP 02Create P2M linkChoose the merchant VPA explicitly, enter amount, and issue a dedicated P2M checkout link. The receiving account's classification is checked before creation.

No bank-issued merchant ID? Use P2P instead. Merchant status cannot be created in software.
