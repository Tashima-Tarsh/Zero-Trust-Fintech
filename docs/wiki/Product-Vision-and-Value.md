# DISHA 8.2 — Merchant-Controlled UPI Intelligence

**The money travels on UPI; DISHA gives the merchant control of the request, trust journey and transaction operations.**

## The innovation

Unlike conventional aggregator checkout models, DISHA is designed as a self-hosted merchant-controlled payment layer: P2P and bank-issued P2M initiation, immutable payment details, order-bound DISHA Vault, Quantum Signal two-way challenge/ACK and UTR/TR correlation. The strategic goal is **payer-side debit-event evidence**, without depending on a merchant notification or an intermediary hosted checkout.

That target is **not yet independently validated end to end**; the Android pre-PIN rejection and authenticatable debit observation interface remain release work.

## Business value

- Direct use of merchant-controlled registered receiving UPI accounts.
- No DISHA software percentage deducted from each payment; intended one-time licensing and implementation arrangement.
- Dedicated merchant SaaS for operations, risk, transactions and genuine reporting.
- Explicit tenant isolation and separately authorized platform-owner administration.
- Distinct P2P and P2M rules, with no invented acquirer details.
- Honest states rather than fabricated dashboard revenue or misleading customer receipts.

## Commercial model

DISHA targets a one-time implementation/software price linked to demonstrated merchant economic value; the payment volume itself is not a DISHA revenue-sharing entitlement. Real infrastructure, statutory charges and bank-specific obligations remain separate.
