THE AGGREGATOR PROBLEM IN INDIA

You worked for the sale. Why let aggregators take a 2% toll?

UPI was designed by NPCI as a sovereign, zero-cost public rail for Indian commerce. But Indian payment gateways repackaged it, inserted themselves as middlemen, and now siphon off your margins while freezing your working capital.

The 2% Margin Bleed

If your business processes ₹50 Lakhs a month, you lose over ₹1,00,000 every 30 days to gateway commission + 18% GST. That is pure profit walking out the door for simple UPI transfers.

T+2 Days Settlement Lock

Your customer paid in seconds, but aggregators hold your cash in their escrow accounts for 24 to 72 hours. Your supplier payments and inventory purchases wait while someone else earns interest on your money.

Arbitrary Gateway Freezes

A sudden surge in sales or a single customer dispute can trigger automated algorithmic freezes on your merchant gateway account, locking your working capital with zero human escalation.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear answers for business owners.

How does DISHA charge 0% commission on UPI?

NPCI built UPI to be a zero-fee public payment rail for peer-to-merchant transfers. Commercial aggregators charge 2% because their business model depends on transaction cuts. DISHA is licensed software you own, meaning we do not take any percentage cut from your sales.

Do my customers need to install any new app?

No. Customers use their everyday UPI applications (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED, BHIM) that they already have on their phones.

How fast do funds reach my bank account?

Instantly (0 seconds). Because payments are routed directly from the customer's bank account to your merchant VPA, there are no batch settlement schedules or T+1/T+2 waiting periods.

How does DISHA protect against fake payment screenshots?

DISHA binds each order to an exact challenge amount and order reference, cross-checking the unique 12-digit bank UTR in real-time. A duplicate or altered screenshot is immediately flagged and rejected.
