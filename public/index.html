STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Do customers need to install DISHA?

No. Customers use compatible UPI applications and the checkout flow they already know.

Do I pay DISHA per transaction?

The proposed licensing model does not charge a percentage-based DISHA software commission. Other applicable charges remain separate.

Do I own the software?

You receive perpetual usage rights to the purchased version under the agreed licence. This does not transfer all underlying intellectual property.

Is payment success guaranteed?

No payment or fraud-prevention system can guarantee every outcome. The console shows its recorded states and supports investigation.
