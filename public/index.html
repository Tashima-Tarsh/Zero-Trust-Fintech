10 / Clear Answers

Questions people ask us

Plain spoken answers without legalistic evasion.

Do you hold my customers' money?

No. DISHA is built to be strictly non-custodial. Payments clear directly to your own receiving commercial bank accounts.

Is there really no commission?

DISHA doesn't charge a percentage of your transactions. Banking, network, hosting, tax and third-party costs are separate, and we itemise them in your quote.

What do I pay, exactly?

A one-time licence and installation price, based strictly on four months of your verified net monthly savings.

What if my volume doubles?

Your DISHA price doesn't change because of it. Your server hosting and operating costs may grow slightly with compute volume.

Will my customers notice any change?

No. They keep paying through standard UPI QR codes, intent requests, and apps (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, CRED) they already use daily.

Can you promise fraud-proof payments?

No one honestly can. DISHA adds layered checks, order binds, and a clear audit record so your ops team can review exactly what happens.

What if there are no savings in my case?

Then we'll tell you right away during the invoice review, and you won't need to purchase DISHA.

Is it compliant with Indian payment rules?

Yes. Built for current NPCI UPI specifications and non-custodial merchant data handling parameters under RBI regulations.
